About us

Built by people who know what the months after treatment ask of you.

Laurel & Oak pairs more than a decade inside addiction recovery with the discipline of running a business well, so every resident comes home to a place that is calm, consistent and kept to a high standard.

RS
Chief Executive Officer

Rikin Shah

Rikin is the co-founder and CEO of GetSure, and began his career in finance at J.P. Morgan and Stone Point Capital. He holds a B.S. in Applied Mathematics from Columbia University and an MBA from the Stanford Graduate School of Business.

At Laurel & Oak he leads strategy, finance and the private platform that keeps residents, families and care teams connected.

JH
Chief Operating Officer

James Hays

James has spent more than ten years in addiction recovery, working one-on-one with clients as a sober coach and on the staff of a residential treatment program.

At Laurel & Oak he runs day-to-day operations: admissions, house standards and the routine that holds it all together. Residents' therapy and clinical care stay with their own providers.
