Questions
Is this treatment?

No. This is a sober living home. Residents keep their own outpatient care, therapy and medical providers.

How long do residents stay?

Most plan for 60 to 120 days, and some stay longer. Billing is weekly, so there's no long lock-in.

Who sees test results and health data?

The resident and house staff. Family or professionals see them only if the resident consents in writing, for the parts they choose, until the date they set. They can revoke it at any time, and they can see who has viewed their information.

Who is it for?

Adults 18 and over who have finished detox and want a structured, quieter place to build the next chapter. Residents on prescribed medication, including MAT, are considered like anyone else.

What does it cost?

One all-inclusive daily rate, depending on a private or shared room. Ask us for current rates and availability.

How fast can someone move in?

Often within days of an inquiry when a bed is open. Tell us the discharge date and we'll plan around it.
