What's included

One all-inclusive daily rate, billed weekly by card or bank autopay. No add-on fees for the basics.

Private and shared rooms

A small house with a handful of residents, furnished and kept to a high standard.

Training and massage

Personal training and massage are part of the stay. Physical recovery is part of recovery.

An Oura ring for every resident

Provided free. Residents see their own sleep, readiness and activity, and can choose to share it with family.

Live-in house manager

Someone in recovery lives in the house, keeps the routine and is there evenings and weekends.

Regular testing

Random and scheduled drug and alcohol testing, with results recorded the same day.

Close to care

Residents keep working with their own therapists, IOPs, psychiatrists and meetings. Petaluma sits within easy reach of Marin and Sonoma programs.

Structure that holds

Clear expectations, applied the same way to everyone.

  • House rules and curfew, signed at move-in
  • Meeting attendance and an active outpatient or recovery plan
  • Random and scheduled testing; results go to the resident first
  • Weekly house meeting and shared chores
  • Residents on prescribed medication, including MAT, are considered like anyone else

Who it's for

Adults 18 and over who have finished detox and want a structured, quieter place to build the next chapter: back to work, to family, to a routine that lasts.

We are a recovery residence, not a treatment program. We don't provide counseling or medical care on site; residents continue care with their own providers.

For families

Questions

Is this treatment?

No. This is a sober living home. Residents keep their own outpatient care, therapy and medical providers.

How long do residents stay?

Most plan for 60 to 120 days, and some stay longer. Billing is weekly, so there's no long lock-in.

Who sees test results and health data?

The resident and house staff. Family or professionals see them only if the resident consents in writing, for the parts they choose, until the date they set. They can revoke it at any time, and they can see who has viewed their information.

What does it cost?

One all-inclusive daily rate, depending on a private or shared room. Ask us for current rates and availability.

How fast can someone move in?

Often within days of an inquiry when a bed is open. Tell us the discharge date and we'll plan around it.
